Tips for Taking Care of Dogs

  • Provide healthy food and clean drinking water.
  • Take your dog for walks and exercise.
  • Bath and groom your dog when needed.
  • Visit the veterinarian regularly.
  • Keep vaccinations up to date.
  • Give your dog love and attention.
